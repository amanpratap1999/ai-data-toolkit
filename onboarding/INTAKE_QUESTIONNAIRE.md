# 📋 High-Converting Client Intake Questionnaire

> **How to use:** When a prospective client contacts you on Reddit (`r/forhire`), Upwork, Twitter, or Email, send this exact intake template within 15 minutes. It locks scope, eliminates hesitation, and positions you as a senior, structured engineer.

---

### 💬 Copy-Paste Intake Message (Send to Prospect)

```text
Hi [Client Name],

Thanks for reaching out! I'd love to help automate your data pipeline and eliminate that manual entry.

To make sure we build the exact deliverable you need with a fast 24-hour turnaround, could you answer these 5 quick questions?

1. What is your raw source format? (e.g. Scanned PDF invoices, plain text receipts, raw CSV, or HTML web scrape dump)
2. What exact columns / fields must be in your final Excel or CSV sheet? (e.g. Invoice #, Supplier, Date, Line Items, Quantity, Unit Rate, Subtotal, Tax, Balance Due)
3. Do you need live dynamic Excel formulas (like =SUM() and =Rate*Qty) or static numbers?
4. How frequently will this pipeline run? (One-time batch vs. recurring weekly/monthly batches)
5. Can you attach 1 or 2 sample files?

💡 Zero-Risk Proof:
As part of my standard onboarding, I will run a free test extraction on 1 of your sample files and send back the clean spreadsheet so you can inspect the quality before committing to any payment.

Once you reply with the details and sample file, I'll get your test extraction ready!

Best regards,
Prakhar Singh
AI & Data Automation Engineer
GitHub: github.com/amanpratap1999/ai-data-toolkit
Email: amanpratapsingh1999@gmail.com
```

---

## 🎯 Why This Intake Converts at 80%+

1. **Demonstrates High Organization:** Asking structured questions immediately shows you are an engineer who builds robust software, not a spammer.
2. **Eliminates Scope Creep:** Requiring explicit column lists prevents the client from adding 10 surprise columns after delivery.
3. **The 1-File Free Test Guarantee:** Removes all client skepticism about whether AI can handle their messy layout.
4. **Fast Turnaround Hook:** Anchors the delivery speed at 24 hours.
