# 💳 PayPal Invoicing Guide & Chargeback-Resistant Templates

> **Goal:** Set up a professional, legally solid invoice template on PayPal in 5 minutes to securely collect $25, $50, and $100 payments with zero friction and robust dispute protection.

---

## 🛠️ Step-by-Step Setup in PayPal Dashboard

1. Log in to your verified PayPal account at [paypal.com/invoice](https://www.paypal.com/invoice).
2. Click **Create Invoice** (or navigate to **Invoicing > Templates > Create Template**).
3. Populate the fields using the standardized templates below.
4. Click **Save as Template** and name it: `"Data Automation Service ($25-$50)"`.

---

## 📝 Exact Field Templates (Copy & Paste)

### 1. Invoice Header
* **Invoice Number:** `INV-AUTO-2026-001` (increment for each gig)
* **Invoice Date:** Today's Date
* **Payment Due:** **Due on receipt** (or Net 7 days)
* **Currency:** **USD ($)**

---

### 2. Line Item Configuration

#### Line Item 1: The Core Automation Pipeline
* **Item Name:**
  ```text
  Python Automation & Data Extraction Pipeline
  ```
* **Quantity:** `1`
* **Price:** `$25.00` *(or $50.00 for batch/multi-source)*
* **Item Description:**
  ```text
  Custom Python software script and data cleaning pipeline tailored to client document specifications. Deliverable includes well-commented source code, environment configuration (requirements.txt), and verified formula-backed Excel spreadsheet (.xlsx / CRM .csv). Delivered electronically via private repository / secure archive.
  ```

---

### 3. Note to Customer (Personal & Professional)
* Paste into **"Note to recipient"**:
  ```text
  Thank you for your business, [Client Name]! 

  The custom Python extraction script and verified sample spreadsheet have been delivered to your email/DM. This invoice confirms delivery and final acceptance of the software service.

  If you need recurring weekly/monthly batch runs or schema updates as your data grows, feel free to reach out anytime!
  ```

---

### 4. Chargeback-Resistant Terms & Conditions (Crucial!)
* Paste into **"Terms and conditions"**:
  ```text
  1. Nature of Service: This invoice covers non-tangible custom software development and automated data processing services delivered electronically.
  2. Proof of Acceptance: The client has reviewed and approved the 1-sample verification extraction prior to final invoice issuance.
  3. Revisions: Includes up to two (2) rounds of schema adjustments requested within seven (7) days of delivery.
  4. Non-Refundable Delivery: Upon electronic transmission of the custom source code and deliverable spreadsheets, services are deemed fully performed and complete.
  ```

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Why this prevents PayPal disputes:**
> When clients pay for digital services, PayPal requires clear evidence that the service was digital, agreed upon, and delivered. The terms above explicitly establish prior verification, electronic delivery, and completed service.
