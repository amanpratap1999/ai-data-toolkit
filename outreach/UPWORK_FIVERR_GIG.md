## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (Copy & Paste to Platform FAQs)

#### Q1: Do you provide the full Python source code?
> **Answer:** Yes, 100%. You receive the complete, well-commented Python source code (`.py`) along with a minimal `requirements.txt` file and clear instructions so you can run the pipeline independently anytime.

#### Q2: Are the Excel formulas live or static text numbers?
> **Answer:** Every spreadsheet is generated with active, native Excel formulas (such as `=C13*D13` for line totals and `=SUM(...)` for subtotals). When you change any quantity or rate, the entire workbook recalculates instantly.

#### Q3: Can your pipeline handle messy or low-resolution documents?
> **Answer:** Yes. The pipeline leverages modern cloud vision and language inference models combined with deterministic regex guards to accurately extract structured fields even from irregular layouts, OCR noise, and messy text dumps.

#### Q4: How is my confidential business data protected?
> **Answer:** All client data is treated under strict confidentiality. Your documents and files are processed in isolated local environments and permanently deleted immediately after project handover. I never share, retain, or train models on client data.

#### Q5: What if I need changes after delivery?
> **Answer:** Every tier includes free post-delivery revision rounds. You have a full 7-day testing window to verify the output on your files, and I will adjust columns or formatting free of charge.
